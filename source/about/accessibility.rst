Accessibility
=============

| To us, accessibility is an important aspect of the modern internet and
| something we consider important to not just talk about but properly support.
| However, it is often difficult to be aware of everything that might make it
| hard for a user to use PufferPanel. Even when aware of an issue, it can still
| be hard at times to judge if something actually helps or only seems to do so
| when the problem does not directly affect you. If you notice any accessibility
| issues with PufferPanel, please reach out and let us know about them. An easy
| way to do that would be `our discord server <https://discord.gg/v8dz49e>`_.
